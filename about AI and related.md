# differences

**Generative AI (Gen AI)** refers to artificial intelligence models trained on vast amounts of data that can create new, original content—such as text, images, code, audio, and video—in response to natural language prompts. Instead of just analyzing or classifying existing data, Gen AI synthesizes patterns to generate entirely novel outputs (e.g., GPT models, image generators like Midjourney, or code assistants).

**AI Agent** is an autonomous software entity powered by AI that can perceive its environment, make decisions, and execute a sequence of actions using tools to achieve a specific goal. Unlike standard chatbots that simply respond turn-by-turn, an AI agent can plan multi-step workflows, call APIs, run code, browse the web, and correct its course if it encounters errors along the way.

**Agentic AI** is the broader paradigm and design philosophy centered on building systems where AI operates with a high degree of autonomy. Rather than requiring constant human prompting for every single step, agentic AI systems use advanced reasoning loops (such as Plan-Execute-Reflect) to break down complex objectives, delegate tasks, utilize tools, and drive projects forward independently.
